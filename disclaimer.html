1. About This Website

Kinglee Mutwiri is an independent storytelling website created to share original articles about people, history, personal experiences, biographies, achievements, challenges and practical lessons that readers may find meaningful in everyday life.

The website focuses on storytelling rather than presenting itself as a government institution, university, medical organization, financial institution, legal organization, news agency or other professional authority.

Our goal is to make information understandable, interesting and useful while respecting the difference between historical information, interpretation, opinion and established facts.

2. Educational and Informational Purpose

The content published on this website is intended primarily for general educational, informational and entertainment purposes. Our articles may discuss historical events, public figures, careers, achievements, social experiences, personal development and lessons that can be drawn from particular stories.

Although reasonable care is taken when preparing articles, readers should not automatically treat every statement on this website as professional advice or as a substitute for information obtained from a qualified professional.

Where a subject requires specialized expertise, readers should seek appropriate professional assistance before making decisions based on information they have found online.

Information can also become outdated as circumstances change. For that reason, we do not guarantee that every statement on the website will always remain completely current, complete or free from error.

When appropriate, articles may identify sources or provide links to organizations and publications where readers can conduct additional research.

4. Historical and Biographical Content

Some articles on this website discuss historical figures, entertainers, athletes, leaders, writers and other public personalities. Biographical articles are created to provide readers with context about a person's life, career, achievements and influence.

Historical storytelling sometimes involves interpretation. Where interpretation is used, it should not automatically be understood as an undisputed historical fact.

We aim to present public figures respectfully and avoid intentionally misrepresenting people. Readers should consult authoritative historical or institutional sources when they require detailed academic research.

We aim to publish original writing created for this website. Our articles are developed around storytelling, research, explanation and practical reflection.

Originality does not mean that every fact discussed on this website is unique to us. Historical facts and publicly known information belong to the broader body of knowledge. What we seek to make original is our presentation, structure, explanation and lessons drawn from the subject.

When external information is necessary to understand a subject, we encourage readers to consult the relevant primary or authoritative source.
