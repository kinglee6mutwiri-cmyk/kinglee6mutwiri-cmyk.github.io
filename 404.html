Money, Behavior, and Financial Decisions

I spend a lot of time analyzing how people handle money because financial success is rarely just about math—it is mostly about human behavior, habits, and mindset. When a person earns more money without changing their underlying habits, lifestyle inflation often leaves them worse off than before.

If you came here looking for my insights on finance, I recommend checking out my article on Wealth Mechanics & Behavioral Strategy, where I break down how compounding and discipline work together.

You might also want to read The Man Who Earned More Money But Became Poorer, which looks closely at why rising income doesn't automatically mean true wealth.
