Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

At Kinglee Mutwiri, I value your trust deeply. When I built this space, I knew that creating a meaningful connection with readers requires transparency, honesty, and mutual respect. I wrote this Privacy Policy in clear, human language to help you understand how I handle information when you visit kinglee6mutwiri.co.ke.

1. Introduction and My Philosophy on Privacy

Welcome to kinglee6mutwiri.co.ke, a personal space I own and independently operate as Eliphas Mutwiri Peter. Here, I share original long-form stories, insightful biographies, practical personal growth tips, thoughtful discussions on finance, and my own everyday observations about human behavior.

When you visit my website, I know it is completely natural to wonder what happens behind the scenes. Even when you are just reading quietly, standard technical protocols exchange small bits of background information. My goal with this policy is to completely strip away confusing legal jargon so I can clearly outline what data I touch, why it matters, and how I protect your reading experience.

This policy applies directly to your interaction with my website, covering anything from direct messages you choose to send me to standard backend analytics and advertising cookies. I always invite you to cross-reference this document with my Terms and Conditions and Disclaimer to get a full view of how I run this platform.

2. Information I Collect

To keep things transparent, the information I collect on this website generally falls into two distinct categories: information you provide to me voluntarily, and information I gather automatically.

Information You Voluntarily Provide

You can read through all my articles, thoughts, and stories without ever creating an account or revealing your identity. However, if you choose to reach out to me via email, phone, WhatsApp, or a contact form, I see that you may voluntarily share details like:

  • Your name or preferred nickname.
  • Your email address.
  • Your telephone number.
  • Any personal questions, article feedback, corrections, or messages you decide to write to me.

I always encourage you to use common sense: I advise you never to share sensitive details (like banking passwords, national identity numbers, or private medical data) through standard web contact forms or ordinary emails unless it is absolutely necessary.

Technical and Usage Information Collected Automatically

Like almost every modern website, my hosting servers and analytics tools automatically collect standard technical information when you load a page. I notice this may include your device type, browser choice, operating system, general geographic region (like country or city), referring web links, and the specific articles or pages you spend time reading. I use this data to understand what topics resonate with my readers so I can continue creating content that matters.

3. Google Analytics and Traffic Insights

To understand how my audience interacts with my site, I use Google Analytics. Google Analytics uses cookies to track visitor interactions anonymously, telling me which stories are popular and how readers navigate through my pages.

This data does not expose your private identity to me; instead, it provides broad statistical patterns. If you prefer to block Google Analytics tracking across the web, I note that you can install the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on provided by Google.

4. Cookies, Web Beacons, and Advertising (Google AdSense)

To support the maintenance and growth of my website, I partner with third-party vendors, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to my website or other websites across the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can direct users to opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

I want you to know that you can manage or disable cookies at any time through your individual web browser settings. Keep in mind that turning off cookies entirely may alter how certain features behave on this and other websites you visit.

5. How I Use Your Information

Every piece of information I process on this website serves a practical, honest purpose:

  • To understand reader behavior and find out which of my articles are most engaging.
  • To monitor website performance, fix broken links, and solve technical glitches.
  • To improve site navigation, layout, and overall user experience.
  • To reply directly when you send me a message, question, or feedback note.
  • To safeguard my platform against malicious bots, spam, and technical threats.
  • To fulfill any legal or administrative obligations if required by law.

6. Contact Communications and Retention

When you take the time to send me a message, I treat your words with respect. I retain correspondence only as long as I find necessary to answer your inquiry, address feedback, or maintain an appropriate archival record. I never publish your private messages publicly without your clear consent.

7. Information Protection and Data Security

Your security is important to me. I host this website securely and serve it over HTTPS, ensuring that data moving between your web browser and my servers is encrypted and shielded from casual interception.

While I take reasonable technical precautions—such as maintaining software updates and keeping hosting systems secure—I know that no internet transmission is 100% foolproof. I encourage you to also practice safe browsing habits on your end.

8. Data Retention Policies

I never keep personal data indefinitely without a valid reason. I save direct communications only as long as I need them for conversation context or administrative records. I handle analytics data based on the automated retention schedules managed by third-party platforms like Google. Once data is no longer useful, I routinely delete or anonymize it.

9. Third-Party Services and External Links

My website features links to external platforms, social media profiles (such as Facebook, Instagram, and X), and third-party references. Once you click an external link and leave kinglee6mutwiri.co.ke, you are subject to the privacy policies and terms of those independent destinations. I hold no control over how external sites process your information.

10. Children’s Privacy

I built Kinglee Mutwiri for a general adult and young-adult audience interested in literature, life reflections, and deep ideas. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has shared personal data with me improperly, I ask that you reach out immediately so I can delete it.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your local jurisdiction, I recognize that you may hold specific legal rights regarding your personal data. These rights can include requesting access to what data exists, asking for corrections, or requesting deletion.

12. Privacy Context in Kenya

Because I operate this website from and reflect a Kenyan creator’s perspective, I strictly adhere to data privacy standards relevant to my region. In accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019), I acknowledge that you retain the right to lodge a formal complaint regarding data handling directly with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) of Kenya via their official channels if you ever feel your privacy rights have been violated.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

As my website grows, introduces new features, or adapts to new legal requirements, I may update this Privacy Policy. Whenever a significant update occurs, I revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page so you always know where things stand.
