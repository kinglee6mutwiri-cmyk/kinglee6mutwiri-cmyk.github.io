Privacy Policy

Stories, Lives & Lessons respects the privacy of people who visit and interact with this website. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, why that information may be collected, how certain third-party services may use information, and the choices available to visitors.

This policy applies to the website available at kinglee6mutwiri-cmyk.github.io and is intended to explain our privacy practices in clear and understandable language.

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. About Stories, Lives & Lessons

Stories, Lives & Lessons is a storytelling and educational website created to publish original articles about people, experiences, biographies, achievements, challenges, careers, culture, personal development and practical lessons that readers can consider in their own lives.

The website is designed for readers who want to explore meaningful stories and information rather than simply consume headlines. Articles may discuss public figures, historical experiences, career journeys, life lessons, educational subjects and other topics that can help readers understand people and the world around them.

Because the website is publicly accessible, visitors may browse pages without creating an account or providing personal information directly. Some information can nevertheless be collected automatically through normal website technology and through services used to understand website traffic and display advertising.

2. Information You Choose to Provide

You can visit most areas of Stories, Lives & Lessons without submitting personal information. However, you may voluntarily provide information when you contact us, send feedback, suggest a story, report an error, ask a question, or communicate with us through another available contact method.

Depending on how you contact us, information you choose to provide may include your name, email address, telephone or WhatsApp contact, message content, subject of your inquiry, and any other information you decide to include in your communication.

We encourage visitors to avoid sending sensitive personal information that is not necessary to answer their question. For example, there is normally no reason to send passwords, financial account details, identification numbers, medical records, or other highly sensitive information when contacting the website about an ordinary article or story.

3. How Contact Information May Be Used

If you contact Stories, Lives & Lessons, information you provide may be used to respond to your message, understand your request, investigate a reported factual error, consider a story suggestion, address a website problem, or communicate with you about the matter you raised.

We may also keep correspondence when it is reasonably necessary for record-keeping, resolving future questions, preventing repeated misunderstandings, or maintaining an accurate history of communication. We do not use ordinary contact messages as a reason to collect unrelated personal information.

4. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit a website, certain technical information can be automatically transmitted by your browser or device. Depending on the technology available and your settings, this can include information such as browser type, device type, operating system, approximate location derived from technical information, pages visited, referring pages, date and time of a visit, and general interaction information.

This type of information is generally used to understand how a website is being accessed and to help maintain, improve and secure the website. It does not necessarily identify you personally.

Stories, Lives & Lessons does not require visitors to create personal profiles simply to read articles. We aim to collect and use information only for legitimate website purposes and services that support the operation of the site.

5. Google Analytics

Stories, Lives & Lessons uses Google Analytics to better understand how visitors use the website. Google Analytics can provide information such as the number of visitors, pages viewed, approximate engagement, traffic sources, device categories and other statistical information.

The purpose of using analytics is to understand which areas of the website are useful to readers, identify technical problems, improve navigation, evaluate content performance and make informed decisions about future website improvements.

Analytics information is primarily used in an aggregated or statistical way. The website does not use Google Analytics as a way of asking visitors to provide passwords or other sensitive account credentials.

Google may process information in accordance with its own privacy policies and technology practices. Visitors who want more information about Google's handling of data should review Google's current privacy documentation and available privacy controls.

6. Google AdSense and Advertising

Stories, Lives & Lessons may display advertisements through Google AdSense. Advertising helps support the operation and continued development of the website.

Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalize advertisements depending on available settings, applicable policies and the choices made by the visitor.

Advertising providers may process information associated with a browser or device in order to understand advertising interactions, measure performance, prevent abuse and provide relevant advertising. The website itself does not ask visitors to submit financial details in order to view ordinary articles.

Visitors may have options to control certain advertising personalization and privacy settings through Google's available advertising controls. Those controls can change over time, so visitors should consult Google's current documentation for the latest options.

Important: The appearance of an advertisement on Stories, Lives & Lessons does not mean that the website personally endorses every product, service or claim contained in that advertisement. Readers should evaluate advertisements independently and use appropriate caution when dealing with third-party businesses.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files or pieces of information that websites and related services may store or access through a browser. They can be used for functions such as remembering preferences, measuring traffic, supporting advertising and understanding how visitors interact with a website.

Some cookies may be necessary for a service to operate, while others may be used for analytics or advertising purposes. The exact cookies available to you can depend on the services being used, your device, your browser and your privacy choices.

Most modern browsers provide controls that allow users to manage, block or delete cookies. Blocking certain cookies can sometimes affect the way websites or advertising services function.

8. Third-Party Services

Stories, Lives & Lessons may rely on third-party services to provide functions such as analytics, advertising, website hosting, security, social media connections or other technical features.

These third parties operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Their practices are not completely controlled by Stories, Lives & Lessons. When you interact directly with a third-party website or service, the privacy policy of that service may also apply.

Examples may include Google services used for analytics and advertising and social platforms linked from the website. A visitor should review the relevant third-party privacy information when using those services.

9. Social Media Links

Stories, Lives & Lessons may provide links to social media profiles or other external platforms. Clicking such a link takes you away from the website and may allow the external platform to collect information according to its own policies.

We do not control how an external social network collects, stores or uses information. Before providing personal information on an external platform, visitors should review that platform's privacy settings and terms.

10. External Links

Articles and pages on Stories, Lives & Lessons may occasionally contain links to external websites when those links are useful to readers. External websites are independently operated and are not controlled by us.

A link to another website does not automatically mean that Stories, Lives & Lessons guarantees the privacy practices, security, accuracy, availability or content of that external website.

Visitors should review the privacy policy of an external website before providing personal information there.

11. How We Use Information

Information available to Stories, Lives & Lessons may be used for legitimate purposes such as:

  • Operating and maintaining the website.
  • Responding to visitor questions and messages.
  • Considering story suggestions and feedback.
  • Investigating reported factual or technical problems.
  • Understanding website traffic and reader behavior.
  • Improving articles, navigation and website performance.
  • Supporting advertising and analytics services.
  • Protecting the website against abuse, spam and security problems.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

12. Information Sharing

Stories, Lives & Lessons does not sell ordinary visitor contact information simply because someone visits the website.

Information may nevertheless be processed by service providers that help operate the website, such as analytics, advertising, hosting, security or communication services. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, protect the website, investigate abuse, enforce applicable terms, or protect the rights and safety of people and services.

13. Data Retention

The length of time information is retained depends on what the information is and why it was collected. Ordinary website analytics may be retained according to the settings and retention practices of the relevant analytics service.

Correspondence may be retained for a reasonable period when necessary to respond to a visitor, maintain communication records, investigate a problem or address a legitimate administrative purpose.

We do not intend to retain personal information indefinitely when there is no reasonable purpose for doing so, subject to legitimate record-keeping, technical and legal requirements.

14. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the website and information associated with it. However, no website, internet connection, email system or online service can guarantee absolute security.

Visitors should therefore avoid sending unnecessary confidential information through ordinary website contact channels.

15. Children's Privacy

Stories, Lives & Lessons is not designed as a service for collecting personal information from children. We do not knowingly request sensitive personal information from children for ordinary website access.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to the website unnecessarily, they may contact us so that the matter can be reviewed and appropriate steps considered.

16. Your Choices

Depending on the technology involved, visitors may have choices about cookies, advertising personalization, analytics and browser settings. You can also choose not to provide personal information when it is not necessary to use a particular website feature.

You may also contact us if you have a reasonable privacy question about information that you voluntarily provided directly to the website. Requests will be considered according to the circumstances and any applicable legal or technical limitations.

17. Accuracy of Information

Visitors should provide accurate information when contacting Stories, Lives & Lessons. If you send a message about an article, including the article title or page address and explaining the issue clearly can help us understand and respond to the matter more efficiently.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website changes its services, technology, advertising arrangements, analytics practices or legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page. The date shown near the beginning of the policy can help visitors identify when the policy was most recently reviewed or updated.
