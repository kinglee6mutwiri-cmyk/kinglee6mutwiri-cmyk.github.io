Contact Stories, Lives & Lessons

Have a question, suggestion, correction or idea for a future story? We welcome thoughtful messages from readers and visitors who want to connect with the people behind the website.

We Would Like to Hear From You

Stories, Lives & Lessons is built around the idea that storytelling works best when there is a connection between the people creating content and the people reading it. A website can publish an article, but readers often notice details, ask questions and provide perspectives that can make future content better.

For that reason, communication is an important part of this website. Whether you have discovered an article that you enjoyed, noticed a factual detail that needs attention, have an idea for a future topic, or simply want to ask a question about the website, you are welcome to get in touch.

We do not expect every reader to agree with every article. Different people can interpret the same story in different ways. Constructive feedback can therefore be valuable when it helps us understand another perspective, identify an issue or improve the way information is presented.

Questions About Our Articles

Readers may contact us about any article published on Stories, Lives & Lessons. If you have a question about a biography, life story, career journey or practical lesson, you can send us the name of the article and explain what you would like to know.

When contacting us about an article, including the article title or page address can make it easier to understand which content you are referring to. If your question concerns a particular statement, mentioning the relevant section can also help us investigate it more efficiently.

We appreciate questions that encourage deeper discussion. A reader may want clarification about a person's career, the background of an event, the meaning of a particular lesson or the sources used when researching a story.

Reporting a Factual Error

Accuracy matters. Stories about real people and real events can contain dates, names, places, career information and other factual details that deserve careful attention.

Although we make an effort to research and review our articles, mistakes can happen. If you believe an article contains incorrect information, please contact us and explain the issue.

When possible, provide information that helps us verify the correction. This might include the relevant article, the statement you believe is inaccurate and a reliable source that supports the correction.

A correction request does not automatically mean that information will be changed. We may need to review the available evidence before making a decision. However, genuine and well-supported corrections are taken seriously because maintaining useful and accurate content is important to us.

Suggesting a Story

Readers often know about interesting people, journeys and experiences that deserve greater attention. If there is a person, biography, career or life story you believe would fit the purpose of Stories, Lives & Lessons, you can suggest it.

A useful story suggestion can include the person's name, the reason you believe their journey is interesting and the main lesson or aspect of their life that you think readers would find valuable.

Suggestions are considered based on relevance, available reliable information and whether the subject fits the editorial direction of the website. Sending a suggestion does not guarantee that an article will be published.

Examples of Useful Story Suggestions

  • A biography of a public figure whose career has an interesting development.
  • A life story that demonstrates perseverance through difficult circumstances.
  • A career journey containing useful lessons about preparation, discipline or change.
  • A historical or cultural story that helps readers understand an important subject.
  • An everyday experience that can be explored for practical lessons.

Feedback About the Website

Contact is not only for problems. We also welcome feedback about the website itself.

If you find an article particularly useful, discover a feature that you enjoy, or have an idea that could make the website easier to use, you can share your thoughts with us.

Feedback about navigation, readability, article organization, accessibility, mobile presentation and other aspects of the website can help identify areas that deserve improvement.

A website should continue to develop as its audience grows. We want Stories, Lives & Lessons to become easier to navigate and more useful to readers over time rather than remaining fixed after publication.

Collaboration and Professional Inquiries

Stories, Lives & Lessons may receive messages from people interested in collaboration, interviews, content projects, research conversations or other professional opportunities.

If you are contacting us about a collaboration, clearly explain who you are, what you are proposing and what you would like to accomplish. Providing enough information in the first message helps us understand whether the proposal is relevant to the website.

We consider opportunities individually. A message expressing interest does not create an agreement or guarantee publication, promotion or collaboration.

Copyright and Image Concerns

We take intellectual property concerns seriously. Stories, Lives & Lessons aims to publish original written material and to use visual material responsibly.

If you believe an image or other material appearing on the website has been used incorrectly or that your rights may be affected, please contact us with the relevant page and a clear explanation of the concern.

Where appropriate, include information that helps establish ownership or the nature of the rights involved. This allows us to review the matter rather than making assumptions based on incomplete information.

We also encourage photographers, creators and rights holders to contact us if they have questions about material appearing on a specific page.

Privacy-Related Questions

If you have a question about how information is handled when you visit the website, you can contact us directly.

For information about privacy practices, cookies, analytics, advertising technologies and related matters, visitors should also review the website's Privacy Policy.

When sending us a message, please avoid including sensitive personal information that is not necessary to explain your question. Only provide information that is relevant to the reason you are contacting us.

What to Include in Your Message

A clear message is usually easier to answer than a very short message with no context. You do not need to write a long email, but providing the important details can help us understand your request.

  • Your name, if you are comfortable providing it.
  • The reason for contacting Stories, Lives & Lessons.
  • The article title or page involved, when relevant.
  • A clear explanation of your question, suggestion or concern.
  • Supporting information or sources when reporting a factual issue.
  • A preferred way to receive a response, where appropriate.

Please remember that providing more personal information than necessary is not required. If your question can be answered without sharing private details, it is better to keep the message simple.

Response Expectations

We read incoming messages and consider them based on their subject and relevance. Response times can vary depending on the number of messages received and the complexity of a request.

A simple question may require less time to review than a detailed correction request, copyright concern or collaboration proposal. Some matters may require additional research before an appropriate response can be provided.

Sending repeated messages about the same request is generally not necessary. If you have already contacted us, allowing reasonable time for the message to be reviewed helps keep communication organized.

Respectful Communication

We welcome different opinions and perspectives. At the same time, communication is most productive when it remains respectful.

Readers are free to disagree with an article or its interpretation. A disagreement can be useful when it explains the alternative view or provides additional information that others can consider.

We therefore encourage visitors to communicate clearly and respectfully, particularly when discussing sensitive personal experiences, public figures or controversial subjects.

For Readers Who Want to Stay Connected

Stories, Lives & Lessons will continue to develop as new stories and articles are published. Readers can follow the website's social channels to discover new content and updates.

You can also return to the homepage to explore the different categories available on the website, including celebrity stories, life stories and articles focused on practical lessons.

The purpose of staying connected is not simply to increase the number of visitors. It is to create an opportunity for readers to discover new stories and continue learning from different experiences.

Our Commitment to Readers

Contacting a website should feel meaningful rather than like sending a message into an empty space. That is why communication is part of the wider responsibility of running Stories, Lives & Lessons.

We want readers to be able to identify who is behind the website, understand its purpose and know how to raise a question or concern. Clear contact information is therefore an important part of building trust.

Our commitment is to continue improving the website's content, presentation and usefulness. That includes listening when readers identify genuine problems and considering suggestions that can make future articles stronger.

No website is perfect, and no article can answer every question. However, being open to correction, learning from feedback and continuing to improve are important parts of responsible publishing.
