Canva AI Tutorial: Design Like a Pro in 10 Minutes

Canva AI is free and perfect for Kenyans. Make posters, CVs, logos without design skills.

Step 1: Sign Up Free

Go to canva.com. Use Gmail. Free plan gives you everything you need.

Step 2: Use Magic Write

Click "Magic Write". Type: "Write a poster for selling mandazi in Kaongo". Canva will write it for you.

Step 3: Use Magic Design

Upload a photo. Click "Magic Design". Canva AI creates 10 poster designs instantly.

Step 4: Magic Eraser

Remove backgrounds from photos. Perfect for product photos and CV pictures.

5 Ways Kenyans Use Canva AI

  • Church posters and event flyers
  • Business logos and price lists
  • CV templates for job applications
  • Social media posts for TikTok and Instagram
  • School projects and presentations

Pro Tip: Search "Kenya" in Canva templates. You’ll find shilling symbols and local designs.
