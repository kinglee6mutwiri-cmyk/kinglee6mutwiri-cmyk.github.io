How Kenyan Farmers Can Use AI to Increase Yields 2026

AI can help you plant better, fight pests, and sell at better prices. Even with a basic phone.

1. Diagnose Crop Diseases

Take a photo of sick maize leaves. Upload to Google Lens or ChatGPT. Ask: "What disease is this and how to treat?"

2. Weather and Planting

Prompt: "Best time to plant beans in Machakos this season"
AI uses weather data to advise you.

3. Market Prices

Prompt: "Current price of tomatoes in Wakulima Market Nairobi"
Know where to sell for best profit.

4. Free AI Tools for Farmers

  • Plantix - Diagnose plant diseases from photo
  • ChatGPT - Ask farming questions in Swahili
  • Google Bard/Gemini - Weather and market info

5. Business Side

Use AI to: write proposals for grants, create WhatsApp marketing for your produce, track expenses.

Tip for Low Data

Use WhatsApp bots. Send photo + "What's wrong with this plant?" to AI bots.

Remember: AI doesn't replace experience. Use it + advice from your local agri officer.
