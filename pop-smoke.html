Pop Smoke: His Life, Music, Legacy and Lessons We Can Apply Today

By Kinglee Mutwiri
Stories, Lives & Lessons
★ Follow us on Facebook for more stories

Pop Smoke's story is one of the most striking examples of how quickly a creative career can develop.

Born Bashar Barakah Jackson in Brooklyn, New York, he became known professionally as Pop Smoke. His career moved from early recordings and local recognition to international attention in an unusually short period.

He became strongly associated with Brooklyn drill, bringing a deep and immediately recognizable voice to music built around heavy rhythms, dark production and energetic performances.

His story is also much larger than the songs that made him famous. It includes childhood experiences, sporting ambitions, experimentation with music, friendships, creative risks, opportunities, pressure, rapid fame and an unfinished future.

This article looks at those different parts of his journey and then asks a more important question: what useful lessons can an ordinary person take from the life of an artist whose career was cut short at only 20?

Who Was Pop Smoke?

Pop Smoke was the professional name of Bashar Barakah Jackson, a Brooklyn rapper who became a central figure in the rise of Brooklyn drill.

He was born on July 20, 1999, in Brooklyn and grew up in Canarsie. His family background included Jamaican and Panamanian heritage, giving him connections to a multicultural New York environment.

He became known for a voice that was unusually deep and powerful for someone his age. That voice quickly became part of his artistic identity.

Instead of taking years to become known, Pop Smoke's rise happened extremely quickly. His first music appeared online toward the end of 2018, and within the following year his name was being discussed far beyond Brooklyn.

His career included the projects Meet the Woo, Meet the Woo 2, and the posthumously released Shoot for the Stars, Aim for the Moon.

Although his career was brief, his contribution to New York rap was significant. Music publications have described him as an important figure in the development and wider visibility of Brooklyn drill. 1

His Childhood in Brooklyn

Pop Smoke's childhood took place in Canarsie, a neighborhood in Brooklyn with a strong Caribbean and West Indian presence.

Growing up in New York meant that he was surrounded by different sounds, personalities, languages, styles and cultural influences.

Before music became his main path, sports played an important role in his young life.

Reports about his childhood describe him as someone who participated in baseball, football and basketball. His ability in sports eventually helped him attend a preparatory school in Philadelphia on a scholarship when he was around 15.

That opportunity did not become a permanent path, however, and he eventually returned to Brooklyn.

His early sporting experiences are important because they demonstrate that his identity was not originally limited to music. Like many young people, he explored different possibilities before finding the field that eventually gave him a public career.

LESSON 1: YOUR FIRST DREAM DOES NOT HAVE TO BE YOUR FINAL DREAM

A young person may begin with one ambition and later discover another ability. Changing direction is not automatically failure. Sometimes life requires you to discover where your strongest opportunity actually exists.

From Sports to Music

The transition from sports into music illustrates an important part of Pop Smoke's story.

A person can possess talent in more than one area, and circumstances can change which talent becomes most important.

Pop Smoke's eventual music career did not follow the traditional path of someone who had spent childhood planning to become a professional rapper.

Instead, music gradually became an increasingly serious part of his life.

He began spending time around recording sessions and eventually experimented with recording his own vocals.

That experimentation became the beginning of a career that would move much faster than he could probably have predicted.

This is one reason his story is useful for young people. Not everyone knows at 15 or 18 exactly what they will become.

Sometimes the important thing is to remain curious, develop your abilities and pay attention when an activity begins to reveal genuine potential.

How He Entered Music

Pop Smoke's entry into music was relatively recent compared with many artists who spend their childhood preparing for a career in entertainment.

His first recorded material began appearing online around late 2018.

At first, he was not an established star. He was an emerging artist experimenting with sound and finding his identity.

The early recordings helped him discover what made his voice effective.

His deep tone immediately separated him from many other young rappers.

He also benefited from working with producers who understood how to build around that voice.

One of the most important collaborations was with UK producer 808Melo, whose production became strongly connected to Pop Smoke's early breakthrough.

The combination of Brooklyn energy and UK drill-inspired production helped create a sound that felt both local and international.

The Rise of Brooklyn Drill

To understand Pop Smoke's importance, it helps to understand Brooklyn drill.

Drill music had already developed important scenes in Chicago and later the United Kingdom.

New York artists began adapting those sounds to their own environments.

Brooklyn's version developed its own character.

The production could be dark, bass-heavy and aggressive, while the artists brought New York slang, accents, local references and individual personalities.

Pop Smoke became one of the strongest representatives of this movement.

His voice made the sound instantly identifiable.

He did not simply reproduce another city's sound. He helped reshape outside influences into something associated with Brooklyn.

LESSON 2: LEARN FROM THE WORLD, BUT BUILD SOMETHING OF YOUR OWN

Success does not require ignoring other people's ideas. It requires learning from them without losing your own identity. You can study successful businesses, athletes, programmers, musicians or entrepreneurs and then develop your own version of what you have learned.

Welcome to the Party

The song that transformed Pop Smoke's career was "Welcome to the Party."

Released in 2019, it became a breakthrough record and helped push Brooklyn drill into wider public attention.

The track stood out because of its heavy production, unusual vocal delivery and energetic atmosphere.

Its success was not limited to Brooklyn.

The song spread across New York and then beyond the city.

It also received remixes involving established artists, increasing its visibility.

The Recording Academy and other music publications have identified the record as a major moment in Pop Smoke's rapid rise. 2

What Does "Welcome to the Party" Represent?

The song is rooted in the street-centered world that influenced Pop Smoke's early music.

Its lyrics contain aggressive themes and language associated with drill culture.

Rather than treating every lyric as life advice, listeners can understand the record as a piece of musical storytelling and cultural expression.

Its most important career lesson may be the way Pop Smoke created an immediately recognizable atmosphere.

The record did not sound like a generic rap song.

It sounded like a specific artist with a specific voice and a specific environment behind him.

LESSON FROM THE SONG: DEVELOP A RECOGNIZABLE IDENTITY

Whether you are creating a business, website, YouTube channel, social-media page or professional career, people should eventually be able to recognize what makes your work different.

Dior

"Dior" became another defining song in Pop Smoke's catalogue.

The song carried the same forceful vocal presence that had helped establish his early identity, while demonstrating that his style could work beyond one breakout record.

Its success helped establish him as more than a one-song phenomenon.

The record also became closely associated with the broader Brooklyn drill movement.

For many listeners, "Dior" represents confidence, energy, ambition and the desire to be recognized.

The Practical Meaning Behind the Song

The surface of the song is about luxury, status and the lifestyle surrounding a successful artist.

But there is another way to examine it.

People often use visible symbols to communicate that they have progressed.

In everyday life, however, genuine progress does not have to mean expensive clothes, jewelry or cars.

It can mean gaining education, building savings, improving your health, becoming more skilled or helping your family.

LESSON FROM "DIOR": DEFINE SUCCESS FOR YOURSELF

Do not allow social media to decide what success means for you. One person may value money, another may value freedom, education, family or business ownership. Choose goals that actually improve your life.

Meet the Woo

Meet the Woo was an important early project in Pop Smoke's career.

It helped turn individual songs into a larger artistic identity.

Instead of being known only for one track, listeners could hear several songs built around the same voice, energy and creative world.

This was important for his development because a sustainable career requires more than one successful moment.

A single viral song can attract attention, but a body of work gives people a reason to continue following an artist.

LESSON 3: BUILD BEYOND YOUR FIRST SUCCESS

If something works once, do not immediately become comfortable. Build the next project. Improve the next skill. Create the next opportunity.

Meet the Woo 2

The second Meet the Woo project arrived as Pop Smoke's profile was expanding rapidly.

By this stage, his audience extended well beyond his original Brooklyn base.

The project demonstrated that his momentum was continuing.

It also showed that he was developing quickly as an artist.

Sadly, the timing of his death meant that listeners never saw the full direction his career might have taken.

His Interest in Growing Beyond Drill

Although Pop Smoke became strongly associated with drill, his musical ambitions were not necessarily limited to one sound.

His collaborations and later recordings showed an interest in expanding his artistic range.

That matters because artists often reach a point where they must decide whether to remain within the formula that made them successful or explore new territory.

Pop Smoke appeared to be moving toward a broader audience.

That evolution can be seen as part of the unfinished nature of his career.

LESSON 4: DO NOT LET YOUR FIRST SUCCESS BECOME YOUR CAGE

A skill that makes you successful today may not be the only skill you need tomorrow. Keep learning even when things are going well.

His Personality and Public Image

Pop Smoke developed a public image that was confident, energetic and sometimes intimidating.

That image was connected to the themes of his music and the drill culture surrounding him.

However, a public persona should not automatically be treated as a complete picture of someone's private personality.

This is an important lesson when consuming celebrity content.

Artists create performances.

Those performances can contain exaggeration, characters, storytelling and symbolism.

A responsible listener can appreciate the artistic work while still separating entertainment from real-life behavior.

The Challenges Behind Rapid Fame

Rapid fame can look easy from the outside.

One day an artist may be relatively unknown, and shortly afterward their songs may be heard across the world.

But the change also brings pressure.

There are new expectations, more attention, financial decisions, professional relationships, criticism and questions about personal safety.

Pop Smoke's career developed so quickly that he had very little time to experience a gradual transition from local artist to international celebrity.

His story therefore reminds us that success is not only about gaining opportunities.

It is also about learning how to manage them.

The Importance of Preparation

One of the strongest practical lessons from Pop Smoke's rise is preparation.

When his opportunity arrived, he already had characteristics that could distinguish him.

His voice was recognizable.

He had developed confidence in the recording environment.

He was willing to work with producers.

He was willing to release music.

He was willing to perform.

These elements meant that when attention arrived, there was something for the audience to discover.

LESSON 5: PREPARE BEFORE PEOPLE NOTICE YOU

Do not wait for success before developing your ability. If you want to become a writer, write now. If you want to become a programmer, code now. If you want to become an entrepreneur, learn the market now.

His International Reach

One remarkable aspect of Pop Smoke's career was how quickly his music crossed geographical boundaries.

His collaboration with UK producers and artists helped connect Brooklyn's sound with international drill culture.

He also traveled outside the United States during his short career.

His sound demonstrated that a strongly local identity can sometimes become an international advantage.

Listeners do not always need an artist to remove every local characteristic.

Sometimes the opposite happens.

A distinct accent, vocabulary, rhythm or cultural identity can make an artist more interesting to people elsewhere.

LESSON 6: YOUR LOCAL IDENTITY CAN BECOME YOUR GLOBAL ADVANTAGE

Do not assume that you must completely imitate foreign trends to succeed. Your own experiences, culture and environment can become part of what makes your work valuable.

His Relationship With Opportunity

Pop Smoke's rise shows how opportunities can appear unexpectedly.

The internet allowed an artist from a particular Brooklyn neighborhood to reach listeners far outside that neighborhood.

Streaming platforms, social media and online communities changed how quickly music could spread.

For modern creators, this is a powerful lesson.

You may not need a traditional gatekeeper to begin.

A person can create a website, publish educational content, start a podcast, build an application, create videos or sell a product with tools that are widely accessible.

But accessibility also means competition is high.

That makes originality and consistency even more important.

His Death

Pop Smoke's life ended on February 19, 2020, in Los Angeles.

He was 20 years old.

He was killed during a home-invasion robbery.

The news shocked the music industry because his career had been developing so rapidly and his future appeared full of possibilities.

At the time of his death, he had only recently released Meet the Woo 2.

The New York State Senate later formally memorialized him, noting his Brooklyn background and the significance of his contribution to music. 3

His death became one of the most painful examples of how quickly a promising life can be interrupted.

Why His Death Changes the Way We Read His Story

Knowing how his life ended changes the way many people listen to his music.

A song recorded when someone is alive can take on a different emotional meaning after that person dies.

Listeners may begin noticing ambition, confidence and references to the future differently.

But it is important not to invent meanings that the artist never explicitly gave.

We can appreciate what the music means to us personally without claiming that our interpretation was necessarily the artist's intention.

That distinction is especially important when writing about historical figures and celebrities.

The Posthumous Album

After his death, his previously recorded work continued to reach audiences.

His album Shoot for the Stars, Aim for the Moon became a major posthumous release and introduced his music to an even broader audience.

The project showed how much material and creative potential remained after his death.

It also reinforced the idea that his career had been interrupted at a point when he appeared to be moving into a larger stage.

The title itself carries a message that can be interpreted as ambition and aiming beyond one's current circumstances.

LESSON 7: THINK BEYOND YOUR CURRENT POSITION

Your present circumstances are not necessarily the limit of your potential. Set goals that challenge you, then break those goals into smaller actions you can actually complete.

The Meaning of His Legacy

Pop Smoke's legacy is closely connected to Brooklyn drill.

He helped bring a local sound into the international mainstream and demonstrated how a distinct regional identity could attract a global audience.

His influence can also be understood through the artists and producers who continued developing drill after his death.

His voice became one of the sounds most associated with the period when Brooklyn drill was gaining international attention.

AllMusic describes him as a major Brooklyn drill figure and highlights songs such as "Dior" and "Mood Swings" among the works associated with his career. 4

Lesson: Confidence Matters

Pop Smoke performed with a strong sense of confidence.

Confidence helped make his music immediately noticeable.

For ordinary people, confidence does not mean believing that you are better than everyone else.

Healthy confidence means trusting that you can learn, improve and contribute.

A student who believes they can improve is more likely to keep studying.

A business owner who believes a problem can be solved is more likely to keep experimenting.

A creator who believes their work can improve is more likely to publish again after criticism.

How to Apply This Today

Before starting an important task, replace "I cannot do this" with "I can learn how to do this."

That small change does not guarantee success, but it creates a more productive starting point.

Lesson: Build Your Own Identity

Pop Smoke's voice became part of his identity.

His sound was recognizable because several elements worked together: his voice, production choices, rhythm and New York identity.

In everyday life, identity is equally important.

If you are building a website, do not make every page look like thousands of other websites.

If you are building a business, understand what makes your service useful.

If you are creating social-media content, develop a recognizable style.

If you are studying, find methods that work for you instead of copying another person's routine exactly.

Lesson: Use Technology as a Doorway

Pop Smoke's rise happened during an era when online platforms could move a song from a local audience to an international one.

Modern technology offers even more possibilities.

A person with a phone and internet connection can learn programming, publish writing, create videos, communicate with customers, study foreign languages or build an online portfolio.

The technology itself is not the achievement.

The important question is what you do with it.

LESSON 8: TOOLS MATTER LESS THAN HOW YOU USE THEM

A smartphone can waste hours or help you build a business. Social media can distract you or introduce your work to thousands of people. Technology becomes valuable when you direct it toward a clear purpose.

Lesson: Do Not Chase an Image at the Expense of Your Future

Celebrity culture often emphasizes expensive clothing, cars, jewelry and status.

Those images can be entertaining, but they can also create unrealistic expectations.

A young person should not measure their worth by whether they can afford the lifestyle shown in music videos.

A stronger definition of success might include financial stability, education, good relationships, health, useful skills and freedom from unnecessary debt.

The positive lesson from an artist associated with luxury imagery is therefore not necessarily "buy expensive things."

It can instead be "work toward a better life while defining what better actually means."

Lesson: Work Quickly, But Think Carefully

Pop Smoke's career developed with remarkable speed.

His story demonstrates the value of acting when an opportunity appears.

However, speed should not mean careless decision-making.

In business and personal life, there are moments when acting quickly is useful and moments when patience is necessary.

The goal is not simply to move fast.

The goal is to move intelligently.

A Practical Method

When an opportunity appears, ask three questions:

What can I gain?

What could I lose?

What information do I need before deciding?

These questions can help prevent excitement from replacing judgment.

Lesson: Protect Your Future

Pop Smoke's death is a reminder that success cannot protect someone from every danger.

Fame does not make a person invincible.

Money does not eliminate every risk.

Young people can therefore learn the importance of personal safety, careful decisions and surrounding themselves with trustworthy people.

This does not mean living in fear.

It means recognizing that your future has value and should be protected.

How Students Can Apply These Lessons

A student can apply the lessons from Pop Smoke's story without having anything to do with music.

Start by identifying one ability you want to improve.

Then create a routine around it.

For example, someone interested in technology could spend 30 minutes each day learning HTML, CSS or programming.

Someone interested in writing could produce one original article every week.

Someone interested in business could study one successful company and identify why customers choose it.

The important principle is consistent development.

How Workers Can Apply These Lessons

At work, being talented is useful, but reliability is equally important.

Learn your responsibilities.

Improve your communication.

Arrive prepared.

Ask questions when you do not understand something.

Keep records of your achievements.

Look for opportunities to learn additional skills.

If a promotion does not happen immediately, do not assume that your career is finished.

Use the time to become more valuable.

How Entrepreneurs Can Apply These Lessons

Entrepreneurs can learn particularly from Pop Smoke's ability to develop an identifiable brand.

A business also needs identity.

Customers should understand what makes a company different.

That difference could be price, quality, convenience, service, trust, specialization or customer experience.

The lesson is not to copy a celebrity's branding.

It is to understand that recognition is valuable.

LESSON 9: MAKE YOUR VALUE EASY TO UNDERSTAND

If someone visits your business or website, they should quickly understand what you offer and why it may be useful to them.

How Creators Can Apply These Lessons

Creators often become discouraged when their first posts receive little attention.

Pop Smoke's story offers a useful reminder that a successful career can emerge from repeated experimentation.

Your first article may not attract many readers.

Your first video may receive very few views.

Your first song may be heard by almost nobody.

That does not automatically mean you lack potential.

The answer may be to improve your skills, study your audience and keep developing your identity.

What His Music Teaches About Storytelling

Music is often remembered because it creates an atmosphere.

Pop Smoke's records were effective partly because listeners could immediately recognize the mood.

This principle applies to writing too.

A good article should not simply provide disconnected facts.

It should give readers a reason to continue.

A strong opening creates curiosity.

Clear sections make information easier to follow.

Examples make abstract ideas understandable.

A conclusion gives the reader something to remember.

LESSON 10: INFORMATION BECOMES MORE POWERFUL WHEN IT IS WELL PRESENTED

Whether you are writing an article, giving a presentation or selling a product, communication matters almost as much as the information itself.

The Importance of Cultural Identity

Pop Smoke's music was strongly connected to Brooklyn.

His accent, vocabulary and references helped listeners understand where the music came from.

That local identity did not prevent international success.

Instead, it helped create a distinctive product.

This is an important lesson for people from every country.

You do not necessarily need to hide your background to appeal to an international audience.

Sometimes your background is exactly what makes your story interesting.

A Lesson for African Creators

African creators can apply this principle to websites, music, business, technology and storytelling.

Instead of trying to make everything look foreign, creators can combine international quality with local experience.

A Kenyan developer, for example, can build technology for a global audience while still understanding problems that exist in Kenya.

A writer can explain an international subject through an African perspective.

A musician can combine global sounds with local cultural influences.

The world is increasingly interested in stories that come from different places.

Why His Story Still Attracts Young Listeners

Part of Pop Smoke's continuing appeal comes from the feeling that his story was unfinished.

He had achieved remarkable progress, but he had not yet reached the point where anyone could know the full extent of his potential.

That creates a natural curiosity about what might have happened next.

His music also remains energetic and distinctive.

For younger listeners, he represents a generation of artists who used internet culture and modern music distribution to move quickly from local recognition to international attention.

What We Should Not Copy

Learning from a celebrity does not mean copying everything associated with that person.

Some themes in drill music involve violence, conflict and risky lifestyles.

Those elements may be part of artistic storytelling, but they should not automatically become behavioral examples.

A responsible lesson is to separate the positive principles from the harmful choices.

We can learn ambition without copying recklessness.

We can learn confidence without arrogance.

We can appreciate music without adopting every behavior described in it.

We can study someone's rise while making safer choices in our own lives.

The Most Important Lesson: Time Is Valuable

Pop Smoke was only 20 when his life ended.

That fact makes his story especially powerful.

Many people assume they have unlimited time.

They postpone education.

They postpone business ideas.

They postpone apologizing to people they love.

They postpone learning skills.

They assume there will always be another opportunity.

No one knows exactly how much time they have.

That does not mean we should panic.

It means we should use our time intentionally.

Do not wait for the perfect moment to begin becoming the person you want to become.

A Practical Daily Routine Inspired by These Lessons

Morning: Choose One Important Goal

Before becoming distracted by your phone, identify one thing that would make the day productive.

During the Day: Practice Your Skill

Spend at least a small amount of time developing something that can improve your future.

Evening: Review Your Decisions

Ask yourself what went well, what went wrong and what you can improve tomorrow.

Weekly: Create Something

Do not spend all your time consuming other people's work. Produce something of your own.

Monthly: Measure Progress

Look at where you were a month ago and compare it with where you are now.

Pop Smoke's Legacy in Perspective

Pop Smoke's career was short, but its cultural impact was larger than the length of his professional life.

He helped push Brooklyn drill into a wider audience and gave the scene a voice that was immediately recognizable.

His music connected New York with international drill influences while maintaining a strong Brooklyn identity.

His posthumous releases further demonstrated the demand for his work.

For historians of modern hip-hop, his career represents a particular moment when regional drill music became increasingly global.

Final Lessons From His Journey

1. FIND YOUR DISTINCTIVE STRENGTH

Identify what makes your contribution different and develop it.

2. START BEFORE YOU FEEL PERFECT

You will learn many things only after you begin.

3. LEARN FROM DIFFERENT PEOPLE

Influence can come from unexpected places. Study widely and think independently.

4. BUILD AFTER YOUR FIRST SUCCESS

One achievement should become the foundation for the next one.

5. PROTECT YOUR FUTURE

Avoid unnecessary risks that can destroy opportunities you have worked hard to create.

6. USE TECHNOLOGY PRODUCTIVELY

The same phone that provides entertainment can also provide education, communication and business opportunities.

7. VALUE YOUR TIME

You cannot recover yesterday. Use today intentionally.

Final Reflection

Pop Smoke's story cannot be reduced to a collection of hit songs.

Behind the music was a young man from Brooklyn who explored sports, discovered music, developed an unusual voice and entered a rapidly changing musical environment.

He found an artistic identity that connected Brooklyn with the wider world.

His breakthrough happened quickly.

His audience grew quickly.

His opportunities expanded quickly.

And tragically, his life ended quickly.

That combination makes his story both inspiring and deeply sobering.

The goal of studying his life should not be to romanticize danger or imitate the most aggressive parts of his musical image.

Instead, we can look at the constructive principles behind his journey.

He developed a recognizable identity.

He experimented until he found a sound that worked for him.

He learned from influences outside his immediate environment.

He took opportunities when they appeared.

He continued building after his first breakthrough.

And his work demonstrated that a person from a specific neighborhood can create something that reaches people far beyond that neighborhood.

For someone reading this today, the practical message is simple.

You do not need to become a celebrity to make your life meaningful.

You do not need millions of followers to develop valuable skills.

You do not need to imitate another person's lifestyle to succeed.

You need to discover what you can become good at, develop it seriously, make intelligent decisions and use your time well.

Pop Smoke's career ended before anyone could see its full potential.

That unfinished story is perhaps one of the strongest reasons to think carefully about our own lives.

There is no guarantee that tomorrow will provide exactly the opportunity we expect.

The best response is not fear.

It is preparation.

Learn today.

Build today.

Create today.

Improve today.

Protect your future today.

Dream beyond your current circumstances, develop your own identity, use your opportunities wisely, and make the time you have count.
