AI Safety Guide for Kenyans: How to Avoid Scams 2026

AI is powerful. Use it safely. Here's what every Kenyan should know.

1. Don't Share Personal Info

Never give AI your: MPESA PIN, ID number, bank details, passwords. Real AI will never ask.

2. Fake AI Apps

Only download ChatGPT, Gemini, Canva from Play Store. Fake apps steal data and money.

3. AI Scams to Watch

  • Voice cloning: Scammers use AI to copy your relative's voice asking for MPESA
  • Fake jobs: "Send 500 KES to get AI job" - It's a scam
  • Investment scams: "AI will double your money in 24 hours"

3. Check Facts

AI can make mistakes. Always double-check important info, especially news and health.

4. Use Official Tools

Free official tools: chat.openai.com, gemini.google.com, canva.com. Bookmark them.

What to Do If Scammed

  1. Call 0722 000 000 - Safaricom fraud
  2. Report to DCI Cyber Crime
  3. Tell your family to warn them

Remember: If it sounds too good to be true, it's AI-powered scam.
