Privacy Policy

Clear information about privacy, communication, website security, personal information and responsible use of Website Eliphas.

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains the general privacy principles followed by Website Eliphas, operated by Kinglee Mutwiri. It explains what information visitors may voluntarily provide, how communication is handled, the importance of protecting sensitive information, and the role of independent third-party services.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Website Eliphas. Your privacy matters, and this Privacy Policy has been prepared to explain in straightforward language how privacy is approached when you visit or communicate with this website. The purpose of this policy is to help visitors understand what information they may choose to provide and what they should consider when interacting with an online publication.

Website Eliphas publishes informational material that may include stories, biographies, historical subjects, personal development, life lessons, ideas and other content intended for general readers. Ordinary visitors can browse the public website without being required to create an account or submit personal information merely to read an article.

The internet is a connected environment in which information can move between devices, networks and service providers. Because of this, privacy involves both the actions of the website operator and the decisions made by individual visitors. This policy therefore explains both sides of that relationship.

The website aims to request as little personal information as reasonably necessary for ordinary communication. If a reader wants to report an error, suggest an article, ask a question or discuss a website problem, the reader should provide information relevant to that purpose rather than unrelated private information.

This Privacy Policy is also intended to distinguish the website itself from independent services used to access or communicate with it. Email providers, messaging platforms, social-media companies, hosting providers, internet service providers and other external services may have their own privacy policies and technical practices.

2. About Website Eliphas

Website Eliphas is an independent website operated by Kinglee Mutwiri. Its purpose is to provide readers with accessible content covering stories, people, achievements, experiences, lessons and ideas that may be useful or interesting in everyday life.

The website may publish pages about well-known individuals and subjects that are already discussed in publicly available sources. Biographical content may focus on important events, achievements, challenges, professional experiences and lessons that readers can learn from.

The website is designed to be accessible to ordinary internet users. Visitors may arrive through search engines, direct links, social-media platforms or other websites. Accessing a public page does not by itself require the visitor to identify themselves to the publisher.

The website may change its design, content categories, navigation and technical features over time. When significant changes affect privacy practices, this policy may be reviewed and updated.

3. Information You Voluntarily Provide

Visitors may voluntarily provide information when they decide to contact the website. This may happen through email, WhatsApp, Facebook or another communication method made available by the publisher.

Depending on the purpose of the communication, a visitor may provide a name, email address, telephone number, article title, message, suggestion or other information included voluntarily in the communication.

For example, a reader reporting a broken page may provide the address of the page where the problem occurred. A reader reporting a possible factual error may identify the relevant statement. Someone suggesting an article may provide the proposed topic and explain why it could be useful to readers.

Visitors are encouraged to provide only information that is relevant to their request. There is generally no reason to provide banking credentials, passwords, identification documents or private account information when asking an ordinary question about website content.

4. Information You Should Not Send

Visitors should never use ordinary website communication channels as a place to unnecessarily disclose sensitive security credentials. This includes passwords, mobile-money PINs, banking passwords, one-time verification codes, card security codes and account recovery codes.

Visitors should also be careful about sending national identity documents, passports, private medical records, confidential business documents or other sensitive material.

If a visitor can explain a problem without attaching a confidential document, that approach is normally safer. If a question does not require a person's full name, there is normally no reason to include additional identity information.

Data minimisation is a useful privacy principle: share enough information to accomplish the purpose, but avoid sharing unrelated information.

5. Email Communication

Website Eliphas provides email as a communication option. The published contact email is kinglee6mutwiri@gmail.com.

When you send an email, your email provider and the recipient's email system may process information necessary to deliver and store the message. The message itself may contain information that you voluntarily provide.

The website publisher may use the information contained in an email to understand and respond to the reason for contacting the website. For example, an email reporting an incorrect article detail may be reviewed while considering whether a correction is appropriate.

Email is not intended to replace secure professional systems designed for confidential records. Visitors should therefore avoid sending highly sensitive information unless there is a genuine reason and an appropriate secure method is available.

7. Facebook Communication

Website Eliphas may maintain a Facebook presence so that readers can follow updates, discover articles and communicate with the publisher.

Facebook is an independent platform. When a visitor visits Facebook, follows a page or profile, sends a message or interacts with content, Facebook may process information according to its own policies and terms.

Website Eliphas does not control Facebook's data systems. Questions about Facebook's processing of information should therefore be directed to Facebook's own privacy resources.

Visitors should avoid posting sensitive information in public Facebook areas. Public posts can potentially be seen by people other than the intended recipient.

8. Public and Private Information

There is an important difference between information deliberately published in a public environment and information sent privately to the website.

If a person publishes their telephone number, address or other personal information publicly on a social-media account, the information may become accessible to other people. Visitors should consider the possible consequences before publishing personal information publicly.

Private communication should also be approached carefully. Sending information privately does not automatically make every type of information appropriate to share.

9. Technical Information

When a browser requests a webpage, ordinary internet communication can involve technical information. Depending on the hosting environment, this can include an internet protocol address, browser information, device information, operating-system information, the requested page and other technical details associated with the request.

Technical information is often necessary for websites and networks to deliver pages, maintain reliability, identify faults and protect systems.

The presence of technical information in normal internet traffic does not necessarily mean that the website publisher personally knows the identity of every visitor.

Hosting providers and network providers may have access to technical information as part of operating their infrastructure. Their own privacy policies may explain their practices in greater detail.

10. Hosting and Website Infrastructure

Website Eliphas depends on internet infrastructure to make its pages available. Hosting involves servers, storage, networks and technical systems that operate independently from the website's editorial content.

A hosting provider may process technical information necessary to deliver pages, maintain system availability, identify errors and protect infrastructure from abuse.

The exact information processed can depend on the hosting provider's systems and configuration. Visitors who need detailed information about hosting infrastructure should consult the applicable provider's privacy documentation.

11. Cookies and Browser Storage

Cookies are small pieces of information that websites or external services can store in a browser. Depending on how a website is configured, cookies may support functionality, remember settings or serve other technical purposes.

Browsers generally provide controls that allow users to manage, delete or restrict cookies. The exact options depend on the browser and operating system.

Changing browser privacy settings can sometimes affect how websites function. A visitor who blocks particular technologies may find that some website features do not operate as expected.

Third-party services can also have their own cookie practices. Their privacy policies should be reviewed when necessary.

12. External Websites

Website Eliphas may contain links to websites operated by other organisations or individuals. When a visitor follows an external link, the visitor enters a website outside the direct control of Website Eliphas.

External websites can have different privacy policies, security practices, cookies and information-processing systems.

A link appearing on Website Eliphas does not mean that the publisher controls the destination website. Visitors should read the privacy policy of an external website before providing personal information to it.

13. Social-Media Services

Social-media links may be provided as a convenience for readers who want to follow the website or communicate through social platforms.

Social-media platforms operate independently and may collect information about visitors according to their own policies.

Visitors should understand that interacting with a social-media platform can involve information processing outside the control of Website Eliphas.

14. Advertising and Tracking

This Privacy Policy is maintained as a policy page and is intended to describe the website's privacy approach accurately. Advertising and tracking services should not be described as active merely because they are common features on websites.

If the website introduces a new third-party service that materially changes how visitor information is handled, the privacy information should be reviewed and updated to reflect the actual implementation.

Visitors should rely on the current website configuration and the current version of this Privacy Policy rather than assuming that a service mentioned on another website is automatically being used here.

15. Information Security

Security is an important part of responsible information handling. However, no internet service can honestly promise absolute security. Websites, devices, networks, email systems and messaging platforms can all experience technical problems.

The website encourages visitors to reduce unnecessary risk by providing only relevant information and protecting their own accounts and devices.

Visitors should keep browsers and operating systems updated, use strong passwords and avoid suspicious links. They should also verify the identity of people requesting sensitive information.

Security is therefore a shared responsibility. Website operators, technology providers and individual users all have an important role in protecting information.

16. Data Minimisation

Data minimisation means limiting personal information to what is reasonably necessary for a specific purpose.

For Website Eliphas, this principle is especially relevant to reader communication. A visitor reporting a spelling mistake does not normally need to provide financial information. A visitor suggesting a biography does not normally need to provide an identification document.

Reducing unnecessary information can make communication simpler and can reduce the potential consequences of accidental disclosure.

17. Data Retention

Information received through communication may be retained for a reasonable period depending on the nature and purpose of the communication.

For example, a correction request may need to remain available while an article is being reviewed. A technical complaint may need to be available while the underlying issue is investigated.

Different communication providers may retain information according to their own systems and policies. The website publisher cannot guarantee the deletion of every copy held by an independent service.

18. Children and Young Visitors

Children and young people should be encouraged to protect their personal information when using the internet.

Parents and guardians can help young internet users understand why passwords, financial credentials, private addresses and other sensitive information should not be shared unnecessarily.

Website Eliphas does not require ordinary visitors to provide sensitive personal information simply to read public content.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has accidentally provided inappropriate information through a website communication channel, they may contact the publisher and explain the concern.

19. Privacy and Biographical Content

Website Eliphas may publish biographies of public figures and other subjects of general interest. Biographical articles may discuss professional careers, achievements, historical events, challenges, public activities and lessons.

The fact that information exists publicly does not automatically mean that every possible personal detail is necessary to publish. Responsible editorial practice involves considering relevance, accuracy and the purpose of the article.

The website aims to focus its biographical content on information that helps readers understand the person's story rather than unnecessarily exposing private details.

20. Public Figures

Public figures may receive substantial public attention because of their careers, achievements or historical importance. Articles about such individuals can discuss matters that are relevant to their public lives.

However, public interest and personal privacy are not always identical. The website should consider whether a particular detail contributes meaningfully to the subject of the article.

Readers who believe that a published biographical detail creates a legitimate privacy concern may contact the website with a clear explanation.

21. Accuracy and Privacy Requests

Privacy concerns can sometimes overlap with questions of accuracy. If an article contains incorrect personal information, a visitor may contact the publisher and explain which statement appears to be incorrect.

A useful request should identify the article, identify the relevant information and explain the concern clearly.

Where appropriate, the publisher may review the information and consider whether a correction, clarification or other action is necessary.

23. How to Make a Privacy Request

When contacting the website about a privacy concern, explain the problem as clearly as possible. Identify the relevant page and describe the information involved.

A request should contain enough information for the publisher to understand the issue but should avoid unnecessary confidential material.

For example, if you believe an article contains incorrect personal information, identify the article and explain which statement you believe is inaccurate. If you believe an image raises a legitimate privacy concern, identify the page containing the image and explain the concern.

Clear requests make it easier to understand the issue and determine what response may be appropriate.

Contributors should not submit another person's private information without a legitimate reason.

Contributors should also avoid submitting passwords, financial credentials, private identification numbers or confidential documents.

25. Responsible Online Communication

Privacy protection also depends on responsible communication. Visitors should think carefully about the information they send and where they send it.

A simple article question normally requires only the information needed to answer the question. A correction request normally requires the article title and explanation of the correction.

The less unrelated personal information included in a message, the less unnecessary information is exposed.

An unexpected request for a password, banking PIN, verification code or other confidential credential should be treated with caution.

Visitors who receive a suspicious message claiming to represent the website can contact the publisher through a known official communication method to raise the concern.

This website should not be treated as a reason to disclose passwords, mobile-money PINs or financial security credentials.
