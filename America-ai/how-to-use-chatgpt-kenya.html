How to Use ChatGPT in Kenya: Full Guide 2026

ChatGPT is free and works perfectly in Kenya. Here's how to start using it today with your phone.

Step 1: Create Account

Go to chat.openai.com. Use your Gmail. Kenyan phone numbers work for verification.

Step 2: Best Prompts for Kenyans

  • "Write me a CV for a job in Nairobi"
  • "Give me 10 business ideas I can start with 10k in Kenya"
  • "Explain this KCSE topic in simple English"
  • "Write WhatsApp messages to get customers for my business"

Step 3: Use on WhatsApp

You can use ChatGPT on WhatsApp through free bots. Search "ChatGPT WhatsApp Kenya" on Google.

Step 4: Make Money With It

Use ChatGPT to write articles, CVs, social media posts, and sell the service on Fiverr or to local businesses.

Note: Free version is enough. No need to pay $20 for ChatGPT Plus yet.
