Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

Kinglee Mutwiri respects the privacy of people who visit this website. This Privacy Policy explains, in clear language, the types of information that may be collected when you use kinglee6mutwiri.co.ke, why that information may be used, how it may be protected, and the choices available to you.

1. Introduction and Our Approach to Privacy

Welcome to kinglee6mutwiri.co.ke, the personal website owned and independently operated by Eliphas Mutwiri Peter. On this platform, I publish original long-form stories, biographies, practical personal growth insights, financial discussions, and observations about human behavior. The core purpose of this privacy policy is to be completely transparent about how visitor information is handled, what data might be processed when you browse these pages, and how your reader experience is respected.

Your privacy matters to me. While visiting any modern website involves the processing of certain technical background data—even when you do not actively submit personal details—this policy is designed to outline those backend processes clearly. I want to ensure you understand exactly what happens behind the screen so you can read, think, and explore the content here with complete peace of mind.

This policy applies directly to the information associated with your use of kinglee6mutwiri.co.ke. It details the text, forms, or messages you choose to share with me directly, alongside the data collected automatically through standard website optimization tools.

This document should be read together with the Terms and Conditions and the Disclaimer. These pages outline other important operational aspects of using this website, including my editorial responsibilities and your rights as a reader.

If you contact the website owner through an available communication channel, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, telephone number, message, question or other information that you decide to include in your communication.

The amount of information you provide is normally your choice. Readers should avoid sending sensitive information through ordinary contact channels unless it is genuinely necessary for the matter they are contacting us about.

For example, if you send an email asking about an article, the message may contain your email address and whatever information you decide to write in the message. That information may be used to understand your request and respond to you.

Technical and Usage Information

Website analytics can collect technical information associated with a visit. Depending on the services being used at a particular time, this may include information such as browser type, operating system, device category, approximate location information, pages visited, referring sources, interaction events and the general duration of a visit.

This type of information is useful because numbers can show how readers actually use a website. For example, analytics can help identify which articles receive attention, whether readers move between pages, and whether a page needs improvement.

3. Google Analytics

Kinglee6mutwiri.co.ke uses Google Analytics to understand website traffic and improve the reader experience. The Google Analytics property currently configured for the website is used for statistical and analytical purposes.

Analytics may provide information about traffic sources, pages viewed, interactions, devices, countries or regions represented in aggregated reporting, and other website usage measurements.

The purpose of using analytics is not to personally identify ordinary readers for its own sake. The information helps the website owner understand how the website performs and where improvements may be useful.

Google operates its own systems and has its own privacy practices. Readers who want detailed information about how Google handles information should review Google's current privacy documentation directly.

Because third-party services can change their features and policies, the way analytics information is handled may also change over time. This Privacy Policy therefore describes the general purpose of analytics rather than promising that a particular technical field will always be collected or stored in exactly the same way.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Websites may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, understand visitor behaviour and remember certain information. Analytics services may also use cookies or related identifiers as part of their measurement systems.

A cookie is a small piece of information that may be stored by a website or service in a visitor's browser. Some cookies are temporary while others may remain for a longer period depending on their purpose and settings.

Your browser normally provides controls for managing cookies. You may be able to block, delete or restrict cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain technologies can sometimes affect how websites or particular features function.

Because Kinglee Mutwiri uses third-party analytics infrastructure, some technologies involved in measurement may be controlled by the third-party provider rather than directly by the website owner.

5. Why Information May Be Used

Information associated with the website may be used for reasonable purposes connected with operating and improving the website.

  • To understand how visitors use the website and which content is useful.
  • To monitor website performance and identify technical problems.
  • To improve article structure, navigation and the overall reader experience.
  • To respond when a reader contacts the website owner.
  • To understand general traffic patterns and audience interests.
  • To protect the website against abuse, malicious activity or technical threats where appropriate.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations when a lawful requirement exists.

The website is not intended to collect personal information simply because a person visits an article. Information is used in connection with legitimate website functions and communication.

6. Contact Information and Communications

If you choose to contact Kinglee Mutwiri, the information you provide in that communication may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to understand the conversation, respond to your request, handle a follow-up matter or keep an appropriate record.

Contact information should be treated carefully. Readers should not send passwords, banking credentials, national identification documents, confidential medical records or other highly sensitive information through ordinary website communication unless there is a clear and appropriate reason.

When a reader asks a question about an article, the message may be used only to understand the question and provide a response. A private communication should not automatically be treated as permission to publish the person's message publicly.

7. How Information Is Protected

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration of information. The website is served over HTTPS, which helps protect information travelling between the visitor's browser and the website.

Website security is not a single feature. It involves keeping software and hosting systems appropriately maintained, limiting unnecessary access, monitoring problems and taking sensible precautions when handling information.

However, no website, online service or method of transmitting information over the internet can honestly promise absolute security. There is always some level of technological risk. For that reason, readers should also take reasonable precautions when sharing information online.

8. Data Retention

Personal information should not be kept indefinitely without a reason. Where Kinglee Mutwiri receives personal information through direct communication, the information may be retained for a reasonable period needed to respond, maintain communication, address a related issue or satisfy a legitimate legal or administrative requirement.

Analytics information may be retained according to the settings and retention practices of the analytics provider. Those periods can change as the provider changes its systems or policies.

When information is no longer reasonably required, it may be deleted, anonymised or otherwise handled so that it is no longer needed for the original purpose.

9. Third-Party Services

Kinglee6mutwiri.co.ke may rely on third-party services to provide parts of the website infrastructure. Analytics is one example. External services operate according to their own systems and privacy policies.

When a third-party service is involved, information may be processed by that provider according to the service's applicable terms. The website owner does not control every technical process performed by an external provider.

Readers should therefore review the privacy information of third-party services when they interact directly with those services or follow links away from this website.

10. External Links

Kingle6mutwiri.co.ke may contain links to websites operated by other organisations, businesses, platforms or individuals.

Before providing personal information to another website, readers should check that website's privacy policy and make their own decision about whether the service is appropriate.

Kinglee Mutwiri may provide links to social media profiles so readers can follow new articles, updates and other public content.

12. Children's Privacy

Kinglee6mutwiri.co.ke is intended for a general audience. The website does not intentionally seek to collect personal information from children.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information through a communication channel connected with the website, they may contact us so that the situation can be reviewed and appropriate action can be taken.

The website does not ask children to provide sensitive personal information as a condition of reading ordinary articles.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply to your situation, you may have rights concerning personal information held about you.

These rights may include requesting access to personal information, asking for inaccurate information to be corrected, requesting deletion where applicable, objecting to certain processing, or asking questions about how information is handled.

Privacy rights are not always absolute. A lawful reason may exist for retaining certain information or refusing a particular request. The applicable law and circumstances of the request will determine what can reasonably be done.

If you believe the website holds personal information about you and you would like to ask about it, you can use the contact details provided in this policy.

Kinglee Mutwiri is a website created and operated from Kenya. Privacy requirements can differ between countries and regions, and different visitors may therefore have different legal rights depending on where they live.

The website aims to handle personal information responsibly and in a manner consistent with applicable privacy requirements. Where Kenyan data protection requirements apply, the website will seek to respect the relevant rights and obligations.

Readers visiting from other countries should also consider the privacy protections available to them under the laws applicable in their own location.

Privacy protection is not only the responsibility of a website owner. Visitors also have an important role to play.

You should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through public or ordinary communication channels. This includes passwords, payment card numbers, banking PINs, authentication codes, copies of identity documents and other information that could create a security risk if disclosed.

If you want to ask about an article or suggest a correction, normally only the information needed to explain your question is necessary.

19. Respect for Reader Communication

When someone takes the time to contact Kinglee Mutwiri, that communication should be treated with reasonable respect. Not every message needs to become public content, and a reader should not assume that sending a private message gives permission for it to be reproduced publicly.

If a message contains an idea, correction or suggestion that may improve an article, the useful information may be considered without unnecessarily exposing the sender's private details.

Readers who specifically want a communication to remain confidential should clearly state that when contacting the website. While reasonable care will be taken, no ordinary internet communication should be assumed to carry an absolute guarantee of confidentiality.

20. Data Minimisation

A simple privacy principle is that information should have a purpose. There is little value in collecting personal information merely because it is technically possible.

Kinglee6mutwiri.co.ke therefore aims to keep the amount of directly provided personal information reasonable for the purpose of the communication or website function involved.

Readers can also help by sharing only what is necessary. If an email question can be answered without a person's telephone number, there is normally no reason to include a telephone number in that message.

21. Website Security and Reader Responsibility

Security involves both the website and the person using it. The website takes reasonable steps to maintain secure connections and reduce avoidable risks, but readers should also use updated browsers, protect their passwords and avoid entering confidential information into suspicious websites.

Kinglee Mutwiri will never intentionally ask readers to publish passwords, banking PINs or authentication codes in a public comment or article.

If you receive a message that appears to impersonate Kinglee Mutwiri and requests sensitive information, do not automatically assume that the message is genuine. Verify the source through an official communication channel.
