1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the website operated under the name Stories, Lives & Lessons. The website publishes storytelling, biographical, educational and informational material intended for general readers.

The purpose of this policy is to describe how information associated with visitors may be handled when they use the website. It also explains the role of third-party services that may operate on or support the website.

This policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions. The Terms explain the general rules for using the website, while this Privacy Policy focuses specifically on privacy and information handling.

2. Information We May Receive

Stories, Lives & Lessons does not require every visitor to create an account or provide personal information simply to read publicly available articles.

However, information may be received in different ways depending on how a visitor interacts with the website.

For example, technical information may be processed automatically when a browser loads a webpage. A visitor may also voluntarily provide information when contacting the website.

The information involved can therefore depend on the particular interaction rather than being the same for every visitor.

3. Information Provided Voluntarily

If you contact Stories, Lives & Lessons by email, WhatsApp or another communication method that we make available, you may choose to provide information such as your name, email address, telephone number, the subject of your message and the contents of your communication.

The information you provide is generally used to understand and respond to the reason for your communication.

For example, a visitor may contact us to report an inaccurate statement, suggest a story topic, ask a question about an article, raise a copyright concern or provide feedback about the website.

You should avoid sending unnecessary confidential information when contacting us. In particular, do not send passwords, banking credentials, payment card numbers or government identification numbers unless there is a legitimate reason and an appropriate secure channel has specifically been provided.

4. Information Collected Automatically

When a webpage is requested, certain technical information may be made available to the website's hosting and technical infrastructure. Depending on the technologies involved, this can include information such as the type of browser being used, device characteristics, operating system, approximate location information derived from network data, referring pages and technical details associated with the request.

This type of information is useful for understanding how a website operates and for identifying technical problems.

We do not use this statement to suggest that every piece of technical information is personally collected by Stories, Lives & Lessons. Different technologies and service providers may process information according to their own systems and policies.

5. Google Analytics

Stories, Lives & Lessons uses Google Analytics to understand general patterns in website usage and to help improve the website.

Our Google Analytics property is configured using the measurement identifier associated with this website.

Analytics can help provide information such as which pages receive visits, how visitors generally arrive at the website, which types of devices are commonly used and whether readers appear to encounter technical difficulties.

The purpose of using analytics is to understand overall website performance rather than to identify individual readers personally through ordinary editorial activity.

Google operates Google Analytics as a separate service and may process information according to Google's applicable policies and technology. Visitors should review Google's own privacy information for a more complete explanation of how Google services operate.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small pieces of information that can be stored by a website or service in a visitor's browser. Similar technologies can also be used to remember settings, measure activity, support advertising or improve website functionality.

Stories, Lives & Lessons may use cookies or technologies provided by third-party services such as analytics and advertising platforms.

Cookies can serve different purposes. Some may be necessary for technical functionality, while others can help measure traffic or support advertising.

Most modern browsers provide settings that allow visitors to control, delete or restrict cookies. Changing browser settings may affect how some websites or online services operate.

7. Google AdSense and Advertising

Stories, Lives & Lessons may display advertisements through Google AdSense and other advertising technologies where appropriate.

Advertising systems can use technologies such as cookies or similar identifiers to help deliver, measure or personalize advertisements, depending on the settings and services involved.

The appearance of an advertisement on this website does not mean that Stories, Lives & Lessons personally recommends or guarantees the advertised product or service.

Advertisers and advertising providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Visitors should review those policies when interacting directly with an advertisement or an advertiser.

Important: Stories, Lives & Lessons does not ask visitors to click advertisements for the purpose of supporting the website. Advertising should only be interacted with when the visitor has genuine interest in the advertisement.

8. Advertising Personalization

Depending on advertising technology, region, browser settings and available controls, advertisements may be personalized or may be shown without personalization.

Personalized advertising can use information associated with browsing activity or interests to make advertisements more relevant. The exact process is controlled by the advertising provider and applicable settings.

Visitors may have advertising controls available through their browser, device or advertising provider. Those controls can differ depending on location and the technology being used.

9. Information About Children

Stories, Lives & Lessons is intended to provide general storytelling and informational material to a broad audience. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise younger users' online activities and to help them understand the importance of protecting personal information on the internet.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to us unnecessarily, they may contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

10. How We May Use Information

Information received through the website may be used for legitimate purposes connected with operating and improving Stories, Lives & Lessons.

Possible purposes include:

  • Responding to visitor questions and messages.
  • Reviewing correction and feedback requests.
  • Investigating technical problems.
  • Understanding general website traffic.
  • Improving article organization and website navigation.
  • Protecting the website against abuse and harmful activity.
  • Maintaining website security.
  • Understanding which types of content are useful to readers.
  • Managing advertising and analytics services.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

11. Communications With Visitors

When you contact us, we may use the information contained in your message to respond to you or investigate the issue you have raised.

For example, if you report a factual error, information in your message may help us locate the article and evaluate the claim. Similarly, if you contact us about a technical problem, details about your device or the page involved may help us understand what happened.

We do not promise that every message will receive a response, but legitimate questions and concerns can be reviewed where reasonably possible.

12. Email Contact

Visitors can contact Stories, Lives & Lessons through the email address published on the website.

Email communications may contain information that you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address and the contents of your message.

Email is not necessarily a secure method for sending highly sensitive information. For that reason, visitors should consider carefully what information they include in an email.

13. WhatsApp Contact

Stories, Lives & Lessons may provide WhatsApp as a convenient method for visitors to communicate with the website.

WhatsApp is operated by a third-party service provider. Messages sent through WhatsApp are subject to the applicable WhatsApp terms, technology and privacy practices.

Visitors should therefore avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through WhatsApp when contacting the website.

14. External Websites

Articles may contain links to websites operated by other people, companies, organizations or services.

Once you leave Stories, Lives & Lessons and visit an external website, that website's own privacy policy and terms may apply.

We do not control the privacy practices of external websites and cannot guarantee how those websites collect, use, store or protect information.

Before providing personal information to another website, visitors should read the privacy information published by that website.

15. Social Media Links

Stories, Lives & Lessons may provide links to social media profiles or pages associated with the website.

Following or visiting a social media link can cause the social media platform to process information according to its own systems and privacy practices.

The website does not control the information practices of those external platforms.

16. Data Security

Reasonable measures are intended to be used to protect information associated with the operation of Stories, Lives & Lessons.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Visitors should therefore understand that information sent through online services can involve risks. We encourage visitors to avoid sending unnecessary confidential information through ordinary contact channels.

17. Data Retention

The amount of time information is retained can depend on why it was received, the nature of the information and technical or legal requirements.

For example, a legitimate communication may need to be retained long enough to resolve the matter or maintain an appropriate record. Technical information handled by hosting, analytics or advertising providers may be retained according to their own policies.

We do not claim that every piece of information is stored for the same period.

18. Third-Party Service Providers

Stories, Lives & Lessons relies on third-party services to support certain aspects of website operation.

These may include hosting services, analytics services, advertising services, communication platforms and other technical providers.

Third-party providers may process technical or other information according to their own agreements, privacy policies and applicable laws.

Using third-party services allows a small independent website to provide features that would otherwise require substantial technical infrastructure.

19. Website Hosting

The website is hosted using online infrastructure that allows its pages and files to be delivered to visitors through the internet.

Hosting infrastructure may process technical information necessary to deliver webpages, detect abuse, maintain security and operate the service.

Visitors should understand that technical processing can occur when any website is requested over the internet.

20. Legal and Security Disclosures

Information may be disclosed where we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to a valid legal request, protect the website, investigate abuse, protect the rights or safety of people, or address a serious security issue.

Such disclosure would be considered in the context of the applicable legal requirements and circumstances.

21. We Do Not Sell Your Contact Information

Stories, Lives & Lessons does not operate as a business whose purpose is to sell visitors' email addresses or WhatsApp contact information to unrelated parties.

Information voluntarily provided when contacting us is used for legitimate website-related communication and handling of the matter for which it was supplied.

This statement does not change how third-party platforms such as email providers, WhatsApp, Google Analytics or advertising services may independently process information according to their own policies.
